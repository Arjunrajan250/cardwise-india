Frequently Asked Questions

Which credit card is best for direct cashback in India?

The SBI Cashback Credit Card is widely regarded as the top option for online shopping, offering 5% cashback on most online purchases without specific merchant tie-ins. For food delivery and groceries, Swiggy HDFC offers 10% on Swiggy services, while Airtel Axis provides 25% on Airtel recharges and 10% on utility bills.

What is a Lifetime Free (LTF) credit card?

A Lifetime Free credit card requires ₹0 joining fee and ₹0 annual maintenance fee for life, with no minimum annual spend condition to waive the fee. Prominent examples include Amazon Pay ICICI Card, Scapia Federal Card, and IDFC FIRST Wealth Card.

How does RuPay UPI credit card linking work?

Credit cards issued on the RuPay network (such as the Tata Neu Infinity RuPay) can be linked directly to UPI payment apps like Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe, or BHIM. This allows cardholders to scan QR codes at local merchants and pay from their credit line while earning reward points.

Editorial Standards & Rating Methodology

How We Evaluate Financial Products

Our review methodology grades credit cards and loans across 5 critical dimensions:

  • 1. Net Value Rate: Base reward rate + accelerated category cashback minus annual fees.
  • 2. Fee Waiver Feasibility: How achievable the annual spend waiver criteria is for median Indian salary tiers.
  • 3. Real-World Usability: Frequency of reward devaluations, points expiry, and redemption options (cashback statement credit vs air miles).
  • 4. Approval Probability: Eligibility criteria realism (minimum income, CIBIL score requirements, FD-backed alternatives).
  • 5. Travel & Lifestyle Perks: Domestic/International airport lounge access conditions and fuel surcharge waivers.
